# What is Meraz
Meraz is the annual techno-cultural fest of IIT Bhilai. It combines technology,
art and culture, with tech showcases, coding battles, performances, fashion and
food.

# What is the theme of Meraz 7.0
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/about/
Meraz 7.0 is the Retro India edition, themed "The Great Indian Reverie", with
the tagline "Rewind. Replay. Rejoice." The theme draws on nostalgic Indian
imagery such as village projectors, roadside trucks and transistor radios.

# How big is Meraz
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/about/
Meraz features over 100 events, draws more than 4,000 participants, and reaches
an online audience of over 2,500.

# When is Meraz 7.0
The exact dates have not been announced on the Meraz website yet. The fest runs
across three days. Follow @meraziitbh on Instagram or check the official
website for the announcement.

# Where is Meraz held
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/contact/
Meraz is held at the Indian Institute of Technology Bhilai, Durg,
Chhattisgarh, India - 491002.

# What passes are available and what do they cost
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/
There are four passes. The Access Pass is Rs 99 for single-day entry excluding
Pronites. The Day 2 Pronites Pass is Rs 299. The Day 3 Pronites Pass is Rs 499.
The Accommodation Pass is Rs 3,499 and covers three days of stay and food.

## Is there a group discount on passes
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/
Yes, for groups of 10 or more. The Day 2 Pronites Pass drops to Rs 249 per
person and Day 3 to Rs 449, each saving Rs 50. The Accommodation Pass drops to
Rs 2,999, saving Rs 500. The Access Pass has no group discount.

## What does the Access Pass include
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/
The Access Pass costs Rs 99 and gives single-day entry to Meraz events on any
one day, not including Pronites. It is for students only and requires a valid
student ID.

## What does the Accommodation Pass include
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/
The Accommodation Pass costs Rs 3,499 and includes three days of accommodation
and food, access to all events, and entry to all Pronites.

## Do I need a pass to enter the campus
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/
Yes. Campus entry requires a valid Access Pass or Student Pass.

## Where do I buy passes
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/
Passes are bought through meraz.ksverse.in, linked from the Passes page on the
Meraz website.

# Who is performing at the Pronites
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/passes/

# What kinds of events are there
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/events/
Meraz events fall into five categories: Culturals, Sci-Tech, Informals and
Varchasva, E-Cell, and FinTech.

# What is the Campus Ambassador programme
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/outreach/
The Campus Ambassador programme lets students from other colleges promote Meraz
and earn rewards based on how many registrations they refer. Meraz 7.0 has 31
ambassadors across 9 colleges, including IIT Patna, NIT Raipur and Manipal
University Jaipur.

## How do I become a Campus Ambassador
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/outreach/
Apply through the Google Form linked on the Outreach page. A full rulebook is
available there as a PDF.

## What do Campus Ambassadors get
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/outreach/
Rewards scale with referrals. At 10 or more you get a free CA pass for the Day
3 concert, two stall coupons, merch and a certificate. At 25 or more you get a
2-day pass covering both concert nights, four coupons and an Instagram
shoutout.

## What are the top Campus Ambassador rewards
url: https://meraz7.vercel.app/outreach/
At 50 or more referrals you get two 2-day passes, eight combo coupons, a gift
hamper, an artist autograph and a bus pool subsidy. At 75 or more you also get
a backstage crew pass, sixteen coupons, a leadership certificate and a memento
on the main stage.
